Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: September 28, 2026

You may request deletion of information associated with messages you sent to the Padayon Rentals Facebook Page.

How to submit a request

  1. Open the Padayon Rentals Facebook Page.
  2. Send the Page a Messenger message stating: Data deletion request.
  3. Briefly identify the Messenger conversation or inquiry you want deleted. Do not send government identification or other unnecessary sensitive information.

What happens next

Padayon Rentals may ask for limited additional information to verify that the requester is associated with the conversation. After verification, an authorized team member will locate information under Padayon Rentals' control that is reasonably associated with the Messenger interaction and delete it when required, unless retention is necessary for a legal, security, fraud-prevention, or legitimate recordkeeping reason. We will respond through Messenger when the request has been handled or if more information is needed.

Information held by other platforms

Padayon Rentals can address copies under its control, including messages forwarded to its internal Discord channel. Meta and Discord operate separate systems and may retain information under their own policies, legal obligations, or backup processes. Padayon Rentals cannot promise deletion from systems controlled solely by those companies. You may need to use Meta's or Discord's own privacy tools for information held independently by them.

Submitting a deletion request does not automatically delete your Facebook or Messenger account.
