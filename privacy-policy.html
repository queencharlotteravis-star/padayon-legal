Padayon Automation

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

About this service

Padayon Rentals operates a Facebook Page and uses Padayon Automation to help authorized team members receive and respond to customer inquiries. When you message the Padayon Rentals Facebook Page, Meta's Messenger Platform may send information from that interaction to Padayon Automation.

Information we receive

Depending on what Meta makes available, the information may include:

  • your name;
  • a Facebook or Messenger user identifier; and
  • the content of messages you send to Padayon Rentals.

Please do not send sensitive personal information that is not needed for your rental inquiry.

How we use information

Padayon Automation forwards incoming customer inquiries to an internal Discord channel. Authorized Padayon Rentals team members use that channel to see the inquiry and respond through the appropriate customer communication channel. We do not sell customer information.

Storage and access

Padayon Automation does not maintain a separate customer database. Information forwarded to the internal Discord channel may remain there so the Padayon Rentals team can handle and follow up on the inquiry. Access is limited to authorized team members. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Third-party services

This service relies on Meta, including Facebook and Messenger, and Discord. Those companies process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their handling of information is outside Padayon Rentals' direct control.

Your choices

You may ask about or request deletion of information associated with your Padayon Rentals Messenger interaction. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or our practices change. The effective date above will show when the current version took effect.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Padayon Rentals by messaging the Padayon Rentals Facebook Page.
